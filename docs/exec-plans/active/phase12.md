# Active execution plan: Phase 12 controlled soft-Coulomb benchmark matrix

## Objective

Turn the now-conditioned finite-AGP evidence into a controlled comparison over
particle number, functional-basis order, and correlation multiplicity without
overstating the structured subclass as generic FEMPS.

## Checkpoints

- [ ] Freeze benchmark schemas, seeds, wall-time/memory measurements, and
  direct-exterior truth limits before launching the matrix.
- [ ] Complete N=4 basis/correlation scans beyond D=10 and K=5 where direct
  exterior reference remains practical.
- [ ] Extend soft-Coulomb scans to N=6 and N=8 with at least two basis orders
  and a reproducible K hierarchy.
- [ ] Compare finite AGP, direct antisymmetric particle TT diagnostics, exact
  diagonalization/CI truth, and the existing ordered-sector route at matched
  small systems.
- [ ] Separate operator quadrature, basis truncation, correlation/optimizer,
  and statistical seed errors in every reported point.
- [ ] Record time, peak memory, retained rank, balanced condition, explicit
  exterior agreement, and antisymmetry diagnostics.
- [ ] Update the novelty matrix before promoting any benchmark figure into a
  method claim.
- [ ] Decide whether the evidence supports a paper-scale structured-FEMPS
  benchmark section or requires another ansatz extension.

## Exit criterion

A reproducible benchmark table must demonstrate independent D and K control at
N=4 and at least one N>4 soft-Coulomb system, with matched truth/diagnostics and
no silent compression or symmetry loss.
