# Hong et al. 2022 - Functional Tensor Network

## Core construction

An orthonormal one-variable functional basis maps a continuous wavefunction to
a coefficient tensor. Linear differential and coordinate operators become
finite matrices in that basis. For many variables, the coefficient tensor is
represented as an open-boundary MPS; norms and operator expectations become
standard tensor contractions, and the Rayleigh quotient is minimized with
automatic differentiation.

## Reproduction interpretation

Figs. 3 and 4 establish basis-order `D` and MPS-bond `chi` convergence. The
paper uses `N=4..16`, caption value `gamma=-0.5`, no three-body term, and reports
an error floor around `1e-5` for finite `D,chi`. Our first run is intentionally
smaller, then scales only after dense/native equivalence is proven.

## Direct FEMPS motivation

The paper says the MPS can be generalized to fermionic tensor networks for
electronic Schrödinger equations, but gives no first-quantized antisymmetric
construction. It also observes that electronic wavefunctions necessarily have
nonzero ordinary particle-cut entropy. FEMPS tests whether this exchange
multiplicity can be made structural rather than an optimizable ordinary bond.
