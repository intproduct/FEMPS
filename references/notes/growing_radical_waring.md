# Growing radical, Waring rank, and Veronese jets

## Scope

This note records the prior-art boundary for Phase 21 candidate I1,
`C[z]/(z^d)`. It is a novelty audit, not a claim that the cited works discuss
FEMPS or fermionic AGP states.

## Established mathematical structure

Carlini--Catalisano--Geramita solve the complex Waring-rank problem for
monomials and give explicit power decompositions
[@CarliniCatalisanoGeramita2012MonomialWaring]. Their formula implies that the
first jet monomial `F_0^(M-1)F_1` has exact rank M for M>=2.

Bernardi--Catalisano--Gimigliano--Ida study osculating varieties of Veronese
embeddings [@BernardiCatalisanoGimiglianoIda2004OsculatingVeronese].
Ballico--Bernardi formulate curvilinear rank using finite schemes on the
Veronese variety and relate it to exact rank bounds
[@BallicoBernardi2012CurvilinearRank]. Bernardi--Brachat--Comon--Mourrain connect
tensor decomposition to truncated moment/Hankel matrices and interpolation
[@BernardiBrachatComonMourrain2011MomentTensor]. Thus jets, powers of linear
forms, and finite-algebra moment functionals are established territory.

## Direct AGP overlap

Dutta et al. rewrite excitation models as linear combinations of nonorthogonal
AGPs. Kawasaki--Gao--Scuseria explicitly describe a border-rank-motivated
small-deformation reorganization of AGP-CI into compact LC-AGP. Phase 21's
coefficient jet is therefore not an admissible new state or contraction class.

## Project-specific conclusion

The new result claimed only inside this project is a negative classification of
the selected matrix-pair coefficient algebra: arbitrary boundaries of
`Omega(z)^M mod z^d` have a constructive exact LC-AGP expansion with at most
`M(d-1)+1` terms. This follows from ordinary univariate interpolation. It is a
useful FEMPS boundary statement, not a new Waring or interpolation theorem.

The exact and limiting notions must remain separate. A length-d curvilinear jet
can have small border/cactus rank while its exact Waring/LC-AGP rank grows with
M. Only the exact interpolation bound satisfies the present contraction gate.
